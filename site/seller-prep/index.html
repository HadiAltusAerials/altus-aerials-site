Getting your home ready for photos

Your photos are the first showing. Most buyers will see your home on their phone before they ever see it in person, so a little prep goes a long way.

A few days before

The bigger stuff

  • Fix the small thingsBurnt-out bulbs, sticky doors, dripping faucets.
  • Mow, edge and clear the yardPut away hoses, toys and yard tools.
  • Put away personal photosAlong with kids' art, mail and anything with names on it.
  • Clear the countersLeave one or two nice pieces in the kitchen and baths.
The night before

Clean and tidy

  • Wipe windows, mirrors and countersThe camera picks up streaks and spots.
  • Make every bedSmooth covers, pillows fluffed.
  • Hide the everydayToys, pet bowls, shoes, laundry baskets, cords.
  • Empty the trashPut bins in the garage or out of sight.
The morning of

Right before we arrive

  • Turn on every lightIncluding lamps and the ones over the stove and vanity.
  • Open all blinds the same wayAll the way up, or all angled the same.
  • Move the carsOff the driveway and away from the front of the house.
  • BathroomsClose toilet lids. Put away soap, toothbrushes and towels.
  • PetsOut of the house if you can, or in one room we'll skip.

During the shoot, we'll move room to room. It's easiest if you step out for a bit or stay in one spot.
