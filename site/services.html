Summit video menu

Pick the video your listing gets

Every Summit shoot includes one short-form video, made for Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Pick one style, or add a second one when you book.

  • Recommended

    FPV Fly-Through

    One continuous flight

    A single unbroken drone flight that starts outside, glides through the front door and moves through the home room by room. Want to be in it? You can greet buyers at the door or appear along the way, but you don’t have to.

  • Highlight Reel

    30 to 45 seconds

    The home’s best moments, cut to music. It opens with a scroll-stopping hook, then moves through the standout shots with the price, beds and baths on screen.

  • Walkthrough

    45 to 60 seconds

    A smooth walk from the front door to the backyard, styled like a film’s opening credits: every room gets its own name card and one detail worth noticing. Want your voice over it? Agent voiceover is included at no extra cost.

  • Agent Intro Reel

    40 to 55 seconds

    You open and close the video and the home fills the middle. You get simple talking points, not a script, and a wireless mic so you sound as good as the house looks.

  • Agent-Led Tour

    60 to 90 seconds

    You walk buyers through the home room by room. The route and key points are planned for you; you say them in your own words. Built for agents who want to be the face of their market.

Every video comes with

  • Vertical 9:16, one file that posts to all three
  • Captions burned in for viewers watching on mute
  • A ready-to-post caption
  • No watermarks
